Shan is a game designer and developer creating thoughtful, aesthetic-driven work that explores the intersection of art, design, and human experience. Based in Los Angeles, working globally.

Her practice spans multiple disciplines including interior design, photography, textile design, and digital art. Each project is an exploration of form, texture, light, and space, seeking beauty in simplicity and meaning in details.
